EE · LV · LT official
One toggle controls all three national radar layers. Estonia uses official KAIA 5-minute historical frames. Lithuania decodes the official Meteo.lt Laukuva + Trakų Vokė animated product into the shared timeline. Latvia uses an official LVĢMC radar animation when one is publicly exposed; otherwise the browser builds a small rolling history from direct LVĢMC frames. No third-party radar mosaic is used.
