Clouds

Daylight imagery & infrared

Sources & attribution

EUMETSAT Meteosat GeoColour / FCI IR10.5 and cloud mask; NOAA GOES visible / longwave infrared; NASA GIBS GOES-East GeoColor / Band 13. Local daylight selects visible imagery; darkness selects infrared. Actual source observation times appear in the layer status.

EUMETSAT ↗NOAA GOES ↗NASA GIBS ↗
Transparent satellite clouds from the eastern US across the Atlantic to Europe and Moscow (85°W–42°E, 25–82°N). GeoColour / visible imagery by local daylight; infrared at night. Only visible tiles load. Wide views use lighter tiles; zoom 5 and closer keep full cloud detail. Play or move the timeline to buffer animation, with smooth transitions. Northern GOES imagery can arrive later; actual observation times appear below. Far Arctic areas beyond satellite visibility stay transparent.

Rain

Radar & rainfall accumulation

Sources & attribution

Radar: Estonian Environment Agency / KAIA, LVĢMC (Latvia), and LHMT / Meteo.lt (Lithuania). Gauge precipitation: Estonian Environment Agency, LHMT, FMI, SMHI and DMI where complete. Open-Meteo rain + showers (CC BY 4.0) fills unsupported countries and gaps. Coastlines: Natural Earth / world-atlas.

Estonia ↗LVĢMC ↗LHMT ↗FMI ↗SMHI ↗DMI ↗Open-Meteo ↗
One toggle controls all three national radar layers. Estonia uses official KAIA 5-minute historical frames. Lithuania decodes the official Meteo.lt Laukuva + Trakų Vokė animated product into the shared timeline. Latvia uses an official LVĢMC radar animation when one is publicly exposed; otherwise the browser builds a small rolling history from direct LVĢMC frames. No third-party radar mosaic is used.

Cyclones

Sources & attribution

NOAA / NCEP GFS model fields for low-pressure centres, pressure, movement, wind, gusts and tracks. NOAA NHC supplies matched Atlantic tropical-storm names. Past tracks contain elapsed modelled positions, not official observed tracks.

NOAA GFS ↗NOAA NHC ↗
Solid trails show elapsed modelled centres, retained across matched model runs as history accumulates. Dashed paths show the forecast with 12-hour steps. Click a rotating centre for pressure, movement, nearby wind and gusts. Use View coverage to find systems across the region.
Closed low-pressure centres and paths derived from NOAA GFS; colours indicate pressure. Includes ordinary Atlantic and European lows. Official Atlantic tropical-storm names are shown when matched to NHC; other lows use tracking IDs. Forecast time is independent of the rain-radar timeline. New model runs every 6 hours; feed checked about every 3 hours. All times are local to your device.

Warnings

Official severe weather alerts

Sources & attribution

Estonian Environment Agency / KAIA, LVĢMC, LHMT / Meteo.lt, FMI CAP, IMGW–PIB, DMI, and MeteoAlarm / EUMETNET national services for the other supported Nordic countries. Country outlines: Natural Earth / world-atlas. Estonian marine warning zones approximate the official named regions.

MeteoAlarm ↗Estonia ↗LVĢMC ↗LHMT ↗FMI ↗IMGW ↗DMI ↗
Shows unexpired warnings affecting today or tomorrow in each country’s local time. Poland uses official IMGW county polygons; Denmark uses official DMI municipality and coastal polygons. Their feeds are copied about every 15 minutes; expired polygons disappear automatically. Latvia uses official LVĢMC warning polygons, updated about every 15 minutes. Finland uses the official FMI CAP service; Finnish marine warnings are shown as dashed sea-zone outlines so they do not imply a land warning on islands inside the forecast polygon.
